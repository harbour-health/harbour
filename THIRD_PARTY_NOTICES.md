# Third-party components

- **Typst 0.13.1**: embedded locally for PDF reports under the [Apache 2.0 licence](https://github.com/typst/typst/blob/v0.13.1/LICENSE). Rust dependencies are pinned in `HarbourReports/Renderer/Cargo.lock`. The compiler has no runtime network or filesystem access beyond the report JSON.
- **typst-assets 0.13.1 fonts**: bundled Libertinus Serif, New Computer Modern, and DejaVu Sans Mono. Full font licence notices are bundled in `HarbourReports/Sources/HarbourReports/Resources/Fonts-NOTICE.txt`.

- **llama.cpp**, pinned build `b10549`: [MIT licence and copyright notice](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/b10549/LICENSE). Prepared frameworks are local, ignored build artifacts; preserve the upstream licence when distributing binaries.
- **Qwen2.5 0.5B / 1.5B / 7B Instruct GGUF**: downloaded from official Qwen repositories. Consult each pinned [Small model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct-GGUF) / [Balanced model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct-GGUF) / [7B model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct-GGUF) for the model licence and attribution before redistribution. Weights are not committed.
- **Health source fixtures**: four paraphrased medication pair entries cite NHS medicine pages; emergency next steps cite Ontario pages. These entries are a limited source fixture, not an official interaction database.
- **Apple platform frameworks and SF Symbols**: supplied by the platform SDK; follow Apple’s distribution terms. Harbour artwork is original to this project.

- **Health Canada Drug Product Database (DPD)**: English/French names and ingredient information derived from the marketed human-drug extract published November 2, 2026. Source: [DPD extract](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-health-products/drug-products/drug-product-database/what-data-extract-drug-product-database.html); [Government of Canada terms](https://healthycanadians.gc.ca/important-eng.php). Modified into a compact vocabulary; not an official Health Canada product or endorsement. Source hashes and transformations are documented in `docs/medication-vocabulary-manifest.json` and `docs/medication-vocabulary.md`. No new interaction or dosing guidance is derived from this name data.
